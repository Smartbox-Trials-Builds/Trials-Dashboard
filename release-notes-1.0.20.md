Adds an automated Device Coordinator Queue to the Shared Dashboard.

- Enter Device Que and Leave Device Queue controls, with a live FIFO waiting list.
- Automatic prep and QA assignments matched to trained devices.
- Expedites are assigned first, followed by the oldest available files.
- Assigned coordinators are removed from the queue automatically, and their names appear on the dashboard.
- Centered assignment notifications remain until Close is clicked, including after an app reload.
- Prevents coordinators from QA'ing their own prep or entering the queue while they have active assigned work.

The database update is already applied to Trials-Dashboard. Install the Windows setup executable or use the app's update controls to receive version 1.0.20.
